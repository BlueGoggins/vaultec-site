§ P Privacy

Privacy.

Who runs this site

This website is run by Vaultec, Switzerland. For any question about your data, write to info@vaultec.ch.

What we collect

Nothing. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, has no forms, and loads no content from third parties — fonts, images, and videos are all served from this site.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web host, GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses to deliver and protect the service. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Your browser

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your own browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device.

Email

If you write to us, we use your address and message only to reply. Our email is hosted by Microsoft 365.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection you can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Write to info@vaultec.ch.
